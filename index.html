Progressive Wave vs Standing Wave

Interactive comparison of particle motion, amplitude, phase and energy transfer

Key Difference

Progressive Wave
Same amplitude + Different phase
The wave profile travels through the medium and transfers energy.
Standing Wave
Different amplitudes + Same phase*
The wave profile remains stationary and there is no net energy transmission along the string.
*Particles between two adjacent nodes vibrate in phase.

Progressive Wave vs Standing Wave

Property Progressive Wave Standing Wave
Wave profile Moves through the medium Does not move
Particle amplitude Same amplitude Different amplitudes
Neighbouring particles Different phases Particles between adjacent nodes vibrate in phase
Nodes No fixed nodes Fixed nodes occur
Antinodes No fixed antinodes Fixed antinodes occur
Particle vibration All particles vibrate Particles at nodes do not vibrate
Energy Energy is transmitted No net energy transmission along the string
Formation Produced by a disturbance travelling through a medium Produced by superposition of two waves travelling in opposite directions

Progressive Wave

y = A sin(ωt − kx)

The displacement depends on both time and position. Therefore, different particles along the medium have different phases.

Two Opposite Travelling Waves

y₁ = A sin(ωt − kx)

y₂ = A sin(ωt + kx)

Standing Wave

y = y₁ + y₂

y = 2A cos(kx) sin(ωt)

The wave profile remains stationary. The amplitude depends on position, producing nodes and antinodes.
